## Notes for the final report / presentation

- Use log-scale y axes when comparing Homa to TCP/DCTCP — the latencies span 3–4 orders of magnitude.
- `fig09` and `fig11` are the two figures that most directly answer **H2 (Flow Control)**: DCTCP does **not** recover Homa's grant-control advantage, and the gap widens under increasing incast pressure.
- `fig07` is the cleanest illustration of **H3 (Operational Overhead)**: one wrong socket option = 291× tail-latency penalty.
- `fig01` vs `fig02`/`fig03` collectively map out the **H1 regime boundary** at roughly 100 KB large-message size.
